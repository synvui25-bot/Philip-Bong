Meet your negotiator

Local insight. Professional care.

I’m Philip Bong, a Senior Real Estate Negotiator helping buyers, sellers, landlords and tenants navigate Kuching’s property market with confidence.

From the first conversation to viewing, negotiation and handover, I keep the process organised, transparent and focused on your goals.

I’m specialising in subsales and rental properties across Kuching, Sarawak.

With 10 years of experience and more than 150 completed transactions representing RM200 million+ in property value, I’ve built my practice around understanding the details behind each client’s decision—not simply the property itself.

I work with new buyers, expat families and investors, offering thoughtful guidance on suitable properties, market considerations and the buying or renting process. I speak English, Mandarin and Bahasa Malaysia, and value clear communication, discretion and long-term relationships.

If you’re considering a property in Kuching, I’m always happy to have a conversation and understand what you’re looking for.
